📱 HOW TO OPEN CROWDCAM ON YOUR PHONE

The missing person form is now working! Here are 3 ways to access it:

METHOD 1: Direct File Access
1. Copy this file to your phone: /home/nur/Desktop/New Folder/cse/crowd_cam/web/index.html
2. Open it with any browser on your phone
3. Tap "Report Missing Person" to see the full form

METHOD 2: Local Web Server
1. On your computer, run: cd "/home/nur/Desktop/New Folder/cse/crowd_cam/web" && python3 -m http.server 8080
2. On your phone's browser, go to: http://[YOUR_COMPUTER_IP]:8080
3. Replace [YOUR_COMPUTER_IP] with your actual IP address

METHOD 3: File Transfer
1. Email the index.html file to yourself
2. Download it on your phone
3. Open with Chrome, Safari, or any browser
